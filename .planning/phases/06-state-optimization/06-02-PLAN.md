<objective>
Eliminate duplicate in-memory copies of employee data in the outreach pipeline.

Purpose: After clustering, the flat `employees` list persists alongside the `clustered` dict (same data in two structures) plus the Kafka serialization buffer. Deleting `employees` after clustering cuts a full copy. Similarly, `all_employees` in the outreach agent persists after being returned to LangGraph state. Explicit cleanup prevents the GC from holding stale references.

Output: outreach_service.py deletes `employees` after clustering; outreach_agent.py clears `all_employees` after returning it.
</objective>
